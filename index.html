“I ship things that are useful first, pretty second — with a preference for boring, resilient engineering.”

I'm a Software Engineering student at Kampala International University, finishing my degree while building and shipping production platforms on the side. The engineering runs deep and wide: Go and Java/Spring Boot services, Next.js and TypeScript front ends, React Native mobile apps, Python for data and machine learning, and Linux, Bash, Docker and PostgreSQL holding it all together.

Alongside that I trained in branding, copywriting and digital marketing through the Goheza program at the National Innovation Hub. That combination shows up in how I build. Chicken City, a multi-vendor marketplace connecting poultry farmers to buyers, feed and machinery sellers, and vets, was designed, built and shipped by me alone — architecture, interface and brand included. Same instinct behind FindMyHome, now serving real listings at findmyhomeuganda.com.

Lately most of my work is AI infrastructure: local model gateways, agent tooling, prompt-driven IDEs and prediction systems.
