About

Two skill sets, one portfolio.

My work sits where backend engineering meets brand craft — because I trained in both, and I don't think a product should have to choose.

I'm a Software Engineering student at Kampala International University, currently finishing my degree while building production platforms on the side. Alongside the engineering — Go, Java/Spring Boot, Next.js, React Native, Python, PostgreSQL — I trained in branding, copywriting and digital marketing through the Goheza program at the National Innovation Hub.

That combination shows up in how I build. Chicken City, a multi-vendor marketplace connecting poultry farmers to buyers, feed and machinery sellers, and vets, was designed, built and shipped by me alone — architecture, interface and brand included. It's the same instinct behind FindMyHome and Gincode: build the whole thing end to end, and make sure it looks like it was designed on purpose.

Before university, I served as Vice President of my school's ICT Club, running workshops and inter-school tech competitions — the first place I learned that shipping software and explaining it to people are two different skills, and a product needs both.
